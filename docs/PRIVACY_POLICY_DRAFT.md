# KidoWorld Coloring privacy policy — draft

**This is a working product draft, not legal advice. Replace every bracketed field and obtain appropriate legal review before publishing.**

Effective date: [DATE]

KidoWorld ("we", "us", or "our") provides KidoWorld Coloring for Kids, a creative coloring game designed for children ages 3–6.

## Child-directed service

The game is designed for young children. Version one is intended to avoid advertising, behavioral profiling, third-party analytics, account creation, chat, social sharing, precise location access and collection of a child's name, email address or contacts.

## Information stored on the device

The game stores artwork progress, preferences and purchase-entitlement status locally on the device so drawings can be resumed. Artwork is not uploaded by the current version.

## Purchases

A grown-up may choose a one-time purchase through Google Play to unlock additional coloring content. Google processes the transaction under its own privacy policy. The app receives purchase and ownership status needed to provide and restore access; it should not receive full payment-card details.

## Diagnostics and platform data

Google Play and the device operating system may provide store, installation, crash or diagnostic information according to the settings and services controlled by Google and the device owner. Before release, this section must be reconciled with the final SDK inventory and the answers submitted in Play Console Data safety.

## Advertising

Version one does not display advertising. Adding advertising later requires an updated policy, child-safety review, Play Console declarations and a compliant app update.

## Data sharing and retention

The current app is intended not to transmit children's artwork or personal information to KidoWorld servers. Local drawings remain until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Store records are retained by Google under Google's policies and legal obligations.

## Grown-up choices

A grown-up can remove locally stored game data using the device's application settings or by uninstalling the app. Purchase restoration is provided through the store account when supported.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information handled by the app. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the game, SDKs, legal requirements or data practices change. We will update the effective date and provide any notices required by applicable law.
